### Scalability & Market Position

Tanjong Sauce (R) is positioned at the intersection of several high-growth trends:

- **FoodTok / Reels Economy** -- Short-form food content is reshaping how consumers discover condiments. Our brand is built for this channel
- **Southeast Asian Flavours Going Global** -- Malaysian and Thai chili sauces are seeing surging demand in international markets
- **Clean Label & Halal Certified** -- Consumers increasingly demand natural ingredients, transparency, and Halal assurance. Our product delivers all three
- **Multi-Channel Distribution** -- E-commerce (TikTok Shop, Shopee, Lazada) + retail + foodservice + export channels create diversified revenue streams
- **Lean & Automated Ops** -- ERPNext, AI content pipelines, and automated workflows mean the business scales without proportional headcount growth
